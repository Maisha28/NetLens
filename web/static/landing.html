What NetLens does

A smoke detector for your network,
not a microscope.

Wireshark shows you everything. NetLens tells you what matters - and proves it.

Live network map

Your devices, this laptop and the internet - with real packets moving between them, named by site and company.

Learns "normal"

Every device gets its own baseline. Alerts mean unusual for this device, not "above some fixed number".

Plain-English alerts

Port scans, heavy data use and impersonation - each with the numbers behind it and what it could mean.

Encryption audit

Grades every secure connection A to F from its cleartext handshake. Flags weak crypto - without decrypting anything.

Tamper-proof evidence

Every alert is sealed in a SHA-256 hash chain with an HMAC signature. Edit one and the chain breaks.

Security score

An A-F grade per device with every deduction explained - and a printable one-page report for an admin.

Tuning lab

Replays a labelled recording under different settings and scores every alert. Thresholds measured, not guessed.

Works anywhere

Live capture of your phone, a built-in simulation, or replay of a saved recording. No internet needed.

How it works
01

It starts as raw bytes.

Every packet your phone sends reaches this laptop as nothing but numbers. NetLens captures them untouched - and never reads a message.

02

Decoded by hand.

Ethernet, IP, TCP, DNS and TLS are parsed with struct.unpack at their RFC byte offsets. No black box: every field traces back to its bytes.

03

Packets become conversations.

Grouped by 5-tuple and named offline - "YouTube · Google", not 142.250.77.14. The map draws itself.

04

It learns what's normal.

Each device builds its own baseline. When one jumps far outside it, you get an alert with the numbers - and attacks can't teach it they're normal.

05

The evidence can't be changed.

Alerts are hash-chained and signed. Quietly edit one afterwards and the chain pinpoints exactly where - try it in the dashboard.
